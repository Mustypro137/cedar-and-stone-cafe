About us

Cedar & Stone is a small, family-run café. We roast our beans in small batches, bake every morning and keep the menu short so everything is made with care.

Pull up a chair, bring a book or a friend, and stay as long as you like.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer vegan and gluten-free options?

Yes. We offer plant-based milks, and several bakery and lunch items are vegan or gluten free. Ask at the counter.

Can I bring my laptop?

Of course. Wi-Fi is free for customers, and we have plenty of power sockets.

Are dogs allowed?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome on our outdoor terrace.

Do you take bookings?

We keep most tables for walk-ins. For groups of six or more, please get in touch using the form below.
